> **Status key:** **Implemented** means the capability exists and has deterministic or documented validation. **Conditional** means it runs only after an analyst approves an eligible real indicator. **Partial** means useful evidence-first coverage exists, but one or more requested sub-items are deliberately not claimed. **Unavailable** means it is not currently part of Signal Furnace.

## Honest SIH Demo Position

Signal Furnace is a strong **email → forensics → intelligence → correlation → evidence → investigation → report** MVP. It is not yet a full commercial mail gateway, DNS intelligence platform, malware sandbox, or person-level attribution system. The next implementation priorities are structured relay forensic records, an evidence-backed investigation graph, private investigation search, richer local spoofing/BEC signals, and durable audit/retention controls.
